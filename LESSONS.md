# Where AI helped, and where it fell over

Notes from building these prototypes and the workflows they come from. I use frontier AI tools daily (Claude, ChatGPT, Copilot, Gemini) for drafting, analysis, research and building.

## Genuinely useful
- **First drafts from structured inputs.** MBR narratives, decision briefs and outreach variants went from 20-40 minutes to a few minutes of review once the inputs were clean.
- **Turning messy requests into structure.** Free-text intake becomes a scored record faster than any form.
- **Building the tools themselves.** These scripts, the HTML map and my websites were built with AI as a pair-programmer; I specify, test and correct.
- **Personalization at volume.** Outreach that references a real signal outperformed generic messages in my own funnel data.

## Where it fell over
- **Arithmetic and totals.** Models mis-added KPI tables and "rounded" numbers. Fix: scripts calculate, models never do the math.
- **Confident gaps.** When data was missing, drafts filled it with plausible text. Fix: prompts require "say the data is incomplete", and the script flags bad rows first.
- **Agents and cost.** Multi-step agents multiply tokens fast. Fix: step limits, model routing and budgets before scaling.
- **Adoption is not automatic.** The tool saved time only after templates, training and a review habit were in place. The verification tax is real in month one and falls with practice.
- **High-stakes people decisions.** Hiring and credit-style decisions stay human until legal review, bias testing and notices are in place.
