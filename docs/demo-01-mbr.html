Summary

What worked

  • Security leaders roundtable, Montreal: $350,000 pipeline from $12,000 MDF (29x the spend).
  • Marketplace listing refresh produced 2 opportunities at no MDF cost.
  • 1 Marketplace transaction closed.

What needs attention

  • Paid social: IT managers spent $9,000 with no opportunities yet.
  • Ransomware readiness webinar had the lowest return: $40,000 from $6,000.
  • Ransomware readiness webinar brought 64 leads but few converted; check lead quality and follow-up.
  • MDF per opportunity ($5,400) is above target.

Suggested next steps (for discussion)

  • Repeat the format of Security leaders roundtable, Montreal next quarter; shift budget toward it.
  • Pause or rework Paid social: IT managers before further spend.
  • Follow up all 124 leads within 10 business days; register qualified ones for co-sell.
